Rujun Han

Senior Research Scientist at Google

LinkedIn Google Scholar

I work on LLM Agents and post-training at Google. My recent work on Deep Research is now available on Gemini Enterprise and Gemini DR Max with millions of users. Previously, I spent two years at AWS AI Labs leading research in RAG, LLM evaluations, and alignment while building enterprise AI assistant services Kendra and Amazon Q.

My recent research spans two complementary directions. On the vertical side, I build agents that solve challenging enterprise use cases in finance, engineering design, supply chain, and more. On the horizontal side, I invent general agent capabilities that enable agents to curate skills and learn from their own experience via recursive self-improvement.
